# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The full canonical text is available at the link above and is incorporated by reference.

## Summary

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone who participates in TAP — contributors, maintainers, users, and observers — regardless of background, experience level, identity, or affiliation.

Project participants are expected to:

- Engage constructively and treat others with respect.
- Assume good faith and respond to disagreement with curiosity rather than hostility.
- Defer to documented technical decisions (see `project-context.md` §2) and propose changes through documented channels rather than relitigating them ad hoc.
- Respect maintainer time: search before opening, follow templates, keep discussion on topic.

Behavior that is unwelcome includes personal attacks, sustained disruption, and any form of harassment as defined in the Contributor Covenant.

## Reporting

To report conduct concerns, email **<conduct@tap.dev>** (forthcoming — track in `project-context.md` §9.6 until provisioned). Reports are handled confidentially by the project's conduct committee, whose composition is documented in `GOVERNANCE.md`.

For security vulnerabilities, follow `SECURITY.md` instead.

## Enforcement

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement ladder:

1. Correction
2. Warning
3. Temporary ban
4. Permanent ban

The conduct committee determines which step applies for a given report. Decisions may be appealed in writing to the project's steering body.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — issue tracker, pull requests, mailing lists, chat channels, conferences and meetups where the project is represented, and any private communication conducted in connection with project participation.
